Character Generation MethodsThe application supports three distinct methods for generating attribute scores:Classic: Roll 3d6 for each attribute in order. (Range: 3–18).Heroic: Roll 4d6, drop the lowest die. Canonical Formatting Rules (Text Generator)The application generates a "Reforged Canonical String" for export based on the following logic:DispositionAlignment is replaced by Disposition. It uses Nouns Only (no adjectives).Correct: "Law/Good"Incorrect: "Lawful Good"Mapping:Lawful Good -> law/goodChaotic Neutral -> chaos/neutralTrue Neutral -> neutral (Single noun)Vital Statistics (Formulas)Hit Points (HP): (Class HD + CON Modifier) * LevelNote: Minimum 1 HP per level.Armor Class (AC): 10 + DEX Modifier + Armor Bonus + Shield BonusBase To Hit (BTH): Floor(Class Progression Modifier * Level)The Output TemplateThe generator strictly adheres to the following sentence structure:"(This [Level][Ordinal] level [Race] [Class]’s vital stats are HP [Value], AC [Value], disposition [Primary]/[Secondary]. Primary attributes: [List of Primes]. [Pronoun] wears [Armor List] and carries [Weapon List], and [Gold Value] gold in coin.)"Example:(This 1st level human fighter’s vital stats are HP 12, AC 16, disposition neutral. Primary attributes: STR, DEX, CON. He wears chain mail and carries a longsword and a large shield, and 5 gold in coin.)
