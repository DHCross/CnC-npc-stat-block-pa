{'header': '## Audience Chamber', 'content': '\n**Role in the Campaign**\n\nWhere one finds powerful men and women, one will find those with less power vying for their attention and favors. Whether the power in question belongs to a King, a lesser noble, a merchant prince, a powerful mage, a jealous godling, or a ruthless cutthroat, almost all of these will have a room or hall in which to meet and lord over their supplicants. The audience chamber is a place where many adventuring parties will find themselves, seeking either work, reward, or mercy.\n\nIt is often the custom for local authority figures to set aside a regular block of time, be it an hour or a day each week, to hear petitions from those subject to their power. Such audiences can range from very formal appearances before a court to simple meetings between the liege and the underling. In many cases, a special room will be set aside to hear petitions.\n\nAudience chambers are often a reflection of the power that holds them. They can be austere, serious places in which the quiet dignity of the power hosting the audience dominates the room. They can be extravagant, seeking to overwhelm petitioners with awe and majesty as they gape at the gaudy display of wealth and power. In some cases, usually associated with extralegal power figures, they can be dark and hidden. The local assassins guildmaster is unlikely to greet petitioners in a rose garden, although just such a setting might put the unwary off their guard…\n\nAudience chambers belonging to the local ruler or legitimate powers tend to be more formal and are usually very busy locales, with lines of potential petitioners gathering early in the morning in the hopes of having their request heard. The person hearing the petitions is invariably surrounded by advisors, guards, servants, and other members of the household, ensuring that the rabble have no chance to harm or otherwise threaten him.\n\n'}
