{'header': '### Role in the Campaign', 'content': '\nIn most fantasy campaigns, much of the adventure takes places beyond the borders of the so-called civilized lands. While adventures do occur in more urban settings, the traditional quests for lost tombs and lairs of vile beasts still tend to be set in wilder, less settled areas.\n\nIn many cases, the remoteness of the location will pose something of a problem to adventurers in search of supplies. Small homesteads or mining camps will rarely have the kind of gear sought after by professional adventurers, and more importantly will not be interested in purchasing or trading for the various trinkets and baubles that adventurers might haul out of the depths of some dank tomb. Into this void comes the trading post.\n\nMuch like the general store in a larger urban setting, the trading post is a multi- purpose business. Often these businesses are part store, part tavern, part post-office, and part community center. For those hardy souls who spend their lives on the frontier, a trip to the trading post is often a much anticipated break from the routine of their lives. Generations of locals likely have met weekly at the local post, exchanged news, griped about the weather, or taxes, or marauding trolls, and sneered at itinerant adventurers.\n\nWhen adventurers first arrive in a new rural locale, it behooves them to seek out a reliable source for equipment, and trading posts are generally good places to obtain hard to find items. Chances are, if the post doesn’t have a mundane bit of adventuring gear, it can get it – though delivery of the item may take time.\n\nThe hardy souls who set off to open trading posts in the more remote corners of the world are not shy, diffident storekeepers. They are often former adventurers themselves who enjoy the steady contact with members of their former profession. Regardless of their past experience, they are almost invariably tough individualists who are willing and capable of defending their small business. Given the nature of most campaign worlds, trading posts will tend to be easily defensible, fortified places.\n\n'}

{'header': '## Role in the Campaign', 'content': '\nThose who travel the roads and rivers of most fantasy worlds can readily attest to the truth of the old saying the nothing in this life is free. In particular, the use of roads and byways is rarely without costs. The trick is finding a toll that enriches the toll-taker without being inconvenient enough to drive business away.\n\nWhile individual tolls might not prove too much of a burden, in particularly populated areas they can become a nuisance.
