Challenge Levels are added to a base number of 12 for primary attributes and 18 for secondary attributes. The player, when required to make such a check, makes a d20 roll and adds their level + attribute modifier, success determined by equaling or beating the total target number. \n\n_Example: a thief of level 2 experience and with 16 dexterity wishes to climb a wall of CL 3.
